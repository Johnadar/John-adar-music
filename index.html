About John Adar

John Adar is a contemporary music artist known for blending modern production with soulful melodies. With over 2 million streams and 50+ live performances, John Adar has established himself as a rising talent in the music industry.

His music spans multiple genres, from indie rock to electronic fusion, creating a unique sound that resonates with audiences across India and beyond.
