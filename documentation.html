How do I book tickets?

Visit the Tour section on the homepage, select your preferred city and date, choose between Gallery or VIP tickets, and complete the payment. Your ticket will be generated immediately.

Can I get a refund?

Refund policies vary by event. Please contact the manager at kshohe11@gmail.com for refund inquiries.

Is my payment secure?

Yes! All payments are processed through Razorpay, a trusted payment gateway with SSL encryption. Your personal information is never stored on our servers.

How do I receive my merchandise?

Merchandise orders are shipped to your address with tracking. Delivery typically takes 5-7 business days within India.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes! You can donate without providing personal information. However, providing your email helps us send you updates about new music and events.
